How to Play

  1. Team A enters the first Malayalam movie title.
  2. The other team must enter a different movie starting with the previous title's last letter.
  3. Each accepted title earns one point for that team.
  4. Do not reuse titles. Skip a turn if the team cannot think of a valid answer.
  5. Malayalam titles are matched by their final Unicode character group; English transliterations are matched by their final English letter.

This version is designed for two teams sharing one device. It does not use a movie database or synchronize across devices.
