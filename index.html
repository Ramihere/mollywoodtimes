How to Play Online

  1. Create a room and share the invite link or room code.
  2. Friends open this website, enter the room code, choose a team, and join.
  3. The team whose turn it is submits a Malayalam movie title for review.
  4. All players on the team whose turn it is can submit a title. The first valid submission is chosen; the opposing team can reject the latest movie if it is not real.
  5. Accepted titles earn one point; rejected titles earn no points and the opposing team takes the turn. The host must keep their browser tab open to process moves and control the timer.

Movie titles are not checked against an external movie database yet. Only share room codes with people you want to join.
